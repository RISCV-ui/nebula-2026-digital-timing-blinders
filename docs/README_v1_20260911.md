# Constraint Optimization through RTL Enhancement Using Generative AI

**Astera Labs Nebula 2026 - Digital Track** - **Team Timing Blinders:** Shubhanshu Shivhare, Nagarjun BV - IIT Bombay

This project closes a real RTL timing loop with an LLM as proposer and deterministic EDA/formal tools as judges. Frozen input RTL is never edited. Every candidate must survive structural validation, formal equivalence or a declared latency contract, CDC checks, clock checks, and final OpenROAD signoff.

## 3W timing diagnosis

The AI and the final report use the same three questions for every timing target:

1. **Which path is causing the problem?** The baseline worst path is in the FP4 dot-product clock domain, through three cascaded `fp8_adder` instances inside `fp4_dot_unit`; signoff reports `clk_s5` WNS **-25.2539 ns**.
2. **Why is it failing?** The path has 195 logic stages and 85.7% of its cell delay lies in the three chained adders. That depth is far beyond the estimated reach of sizing or buffering alone. The later `clk1` and `clk_s8` misses were only 0.166/0.149 ns and came through the DMA/interconnect cone, so those were physical-repair targets rather than reasons to alter functionality.
3. **What is the solution?** At RTL, restructure `fp8_adder` and pipeline the elastic FP4 stream with a declared +4-cycle latency, then prove data/order preservation. At tool level, repair fanout/slew, resize cells, insert buffers, and route again. The final physical pass used `repair_timing -repair_tns 100 -setup_margin 0.2`; it closed `clk1` and `clk_s8` without changing RTL.

This separation prevents the LLM from proposing a risky RTL rewrite for a path that ordinary physical optimization can close cheaply.

## Honest limits

- `clk2` through `clk5` report no timing paths and are not counted as passing.
- Four timed clocks regress in slack while remaining closed.
- Setup is closed, but one **-0.0231 ns hold violation** remains and is reported separately.
- Unrestricted EQY leaves 10 timeout, 7 unproven and 1 tool-error module. Reset-aware bounded proofs close ten of those gaps; 8 modules still lack a proof.
- Five brute-force deeper retries produced zero new proofs. The separate reset-aware method reached 46/54 and rejected an inverted-output negative control for every added proof.
- Paid model arm and demo video remain pending.
